How it works

Three steps from a username to a clickable promotion.

1

Enter a username

A creator or streamer types the name of the person they want to promote.

2

TOEFL builds the promotion

It generates a profile card with the name, a picture and a Visit Profile link.

3

Viewers click through

The card appears on screen. One click takes viewers straight to that profile.

Questions

Straight answers.

Is this an official X, TikTok or Instagram integration?

No. This is a demonstration. Official integrations are planned and would be added through each platform's approved tools.

Which platform works today?

The demo creates links to X profiles in the form https://x.com/username.

What can I type as a username?

Between 1 and 15 letters, numbers or underscores. A leading @ is removed for you.

Can I use my own video?

The demo character is built into this page. A custom video option is planned.
